    Lost something on campus? Found something that belongs to someone?

    FindIT replaces scattered noticeboards and group chats with one searchable, auditable lost and found workflow. Report an item, review suggested matches and let the campus office verify and hand items back safely.

    Create an account Sign in Browse reports

    How it works

    1. Report a lost or found item with a category, location and date.
    2. Get rule-based match suggestions that explain how the score was calculated.
    3. Submit a private claim with the details only the owner knows.
    4. An administrator verifies the claim and records the handover.
    🔍

    Searchable by design

    Filter by lost or found, category, campus location and date. Results are paginated and stable.

    🔒

    Private by default

    Claim explanations, verification answers and contact details stay private. Only the claimant and administrators can read them.

    ⚖✓

    Verified handover

    A match score is only a suggestion. An administrator verifies ownership and records every decision.
