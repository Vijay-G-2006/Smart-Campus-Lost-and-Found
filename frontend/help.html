    How FindIT works and what happens with your data.

    Frequently asked questions

    How are match scores calculated?

    The score is rule based and deterministic: the same category is worth 30 points, shared keywords in the title and description are worth up to 30 points, the location is worth up to 20 points and the date distance is worth up to 20 points. The maximum is 100 points. The breakdown is shown with every suggestion.

    Does a high score prove that an item is mine?

    No. A score is only a suggestion. Ownership is always verified manually by a campus administrator, and only the administrator can approve a claim.

    Who can see my claim?

    Only you and the administrators who review claims. Your explanation, verification answers and contact details never appear in a public report and are not visible to other students.

    Who can see the private verification detail of a report?

    Only the person who created the report and the administrators. It is used to compare a claim with the item, for example to check the contents of a bag.

    Can I still edit my report?

    You can edit or close your own report while it is still open. As soon as a claim is being reviewed or the item was returned, the report is locked so that the history stays correct.

    What happens after a claim is approved?

    The administrator coordinates the handover and then marks the item as returned. From that moment the report does not accept new claims and the status history is final.

    How do I report an item that is not in a category?

    Use the category "Other" and describe the item as precisely as possible. A detailed description improves the keyword matching.

    Privacy notice
    • FindIT stores the display name, email address and the reports you create.
    • Passwords are stored as a one-way hash (BCrypt) and are never readable.
    • Uploaded images are stored in a private location and are only served to signed-in users.
    • Claim texts, verification answers and contact details are private and are only visible to you and to administrators.
    • Administrative actions are recorded in an audit log so that decisions stay traceable.
    • Do not enter sensitive personal data such as national identity numbers or payment information.
    Accessibility

    FindIT uses semantic HTML, labelled form controls, visible focus states and status labels that never rely on colour alone. The layout works on phones, tablets and desktops.

    If you find a barrier, please report it to the campus lost property office so that it can be fixed.
